My Journey into AI and Machine Learning

Published on December 15, 2024 by David Pham

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning have completely transformed how I think about technology and problem-solving. What started as curiosity about how computers could "think" has evolved into a passionate journey of discovery and innovation.

The Beginning

My first encounter with AI was during my second year at the University of Alberta. I was working on a computer vision project that required object detection, and I was amazed by how neural networks could identify patterns that seemed impossible to define with traditional programming.

Key Learning Moments

Throughout my journey, several moments stood out as pivotal learning experiences:

  • Understanding Neural Networks: The "aha!" moment when I finally grasped how backpropagation works
  • First Computer Vision Project: Building an image classifier that could identify different types of objects
  • Real-world Applications: Seeing how AI could solve actual problems in industries like healthcare and finance
  • Ethical Considerations: Learning about bias, fairness, and the responsibility that comes with AI development

Technical Skills Developed

Over the years, I've developed proficiency in various AI/ML technologies and frameworks:

Programming Languages

  • Python - My go-to language for ML projects
  • R - For statistical analysis and data science
  • C++ - For performance-critical applications

Frameworks and Libraries

  • TensorFlow and Keras - For deep learning projects
  • OpenCV - For computer vision applications
  • Scikit-learn - For traditional machine learning algorithms
  • NumPy and Pandas - For data manipulation and analysis

Current Focus Areas

Right now, I'm particularly interested in:

  • Computer Vision: Working on object detection and image segmentation projects
  • Robotics: Integrating AI with robotic systems for autonomous navigation
  • Edge Computing: Optimizing ML models to run efficiently on resource-constrained devices
  • Ethical AI: Developing fair and unbiased AI systems

Looking Forward

The field of AI is evolving at an incredible pace, and I'm excited to be part of this transformation. My goal is to contribute to developing AI systems that not only solve complex technical problems but also make a positive impact on society.

Whether it's through my internship at Superstars Inc., my academic projects at the University of Alberta, or personal side projects, I'm constantly learning and pushing the boundaries of what's possible with AI.

If you're interested in AI or have any questions about my journey, feel free to reach out! I'm always happy to discuss the fascinating world of artificial intelligence and machine learning.
